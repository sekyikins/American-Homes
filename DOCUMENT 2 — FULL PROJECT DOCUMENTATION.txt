5. CORE SYSTEM PRINCIPLES
1. Ledger-based truth

Nothing is overwritten — everything is recorded.

2. Batch-based inventory

Every item belongs to a shipment batch.

3. Wallet-based commission

Agents earn from internal wallet system.

4. Role-based access

Every staff sees only what they need.

5. Offline-first POS

Sales must not fail due to internet issues.
